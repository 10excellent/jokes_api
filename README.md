## 😂 Bad Jokes: the jokes app that needs no download

[![Bad Jokes: one joke per screen, vote haha or meh](https://jokes.10excellent.com/assets/img/app/en/jokes-app-one-joke-per-screen.webp)](https://jokes.10excellent.com/)

**[Bad Jokes 10excellent](https://jokes.10excellent.com/)** is a free jokes app that runs right in your browser.
One joke per screen: read the setup, guess the punchline, tap, then vote haha or meh.
Readers' votes lift the funniest jokes to the top.

- **Dad jokes, puns, one-liners, knock-knock jokes and funny stories**, sorted by topic
- **Didn't get the joke?** Tricky puns come with a one-line explanation
- **Jokes read aloud**, with autoplay for hands-free laughs
- **Joke of the day** every morning, by push or email
- **Clean jokes for kids** with one filter; adult jokes stay behind an age check
- **Share any joke as a picture**, ready for chats and stories
- **Works offline** and installs on any phone or computer as an app (PWA)
- Also in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Polish and Japanese

👉 **[Open the jokes](https://jokes.10excellent.com/)** · [How to install the jokes app](https://jokes.10excellent.com/info/jokes-app)
